# Photo provenance

The photographs in this folder come from three sources:

1. **cpu-ns32k.net**, Udo Möller's Series 32000 site:
   `MX2_inside.jpg` and `97801_board_DSCN4117.jpg`.
2. **Plamen Mihaylov** (MAME developer), direct photographs of the PC-MX2
   boards and systems in his own collection, supplied for this preservation
   work.
3. **Web-based archival sources**, the OldComputers PC-MX2 mirror (photos
   bundled with the ROM dumps) and community postings.
